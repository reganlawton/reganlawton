## Craft CMS

Craft CMS has been a big part of my development work in the early years. I maintain a small collection of open-source plugins under [wrav](https://github.com/wrav), including:

- [oEmbed](https://github.com/wrav/oembed) - fetches media information from services such as YouTube and other websites using the oEmbed protocol, used by over 7k+ sites
- [Related](https://github.com/wrav/related) - adds quick access to related entries in the Craft control panel, giving you a better understanding of your content
- [SimpleSharing](https://github.com/wrav/SimpleSharing) - simple social sharing tools for Craft CMS, one click links to share on Twitter/X, Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.
